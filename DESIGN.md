# Clyra visual identity

Finalised 5 November 2026 for the website and project pitch PDF.

The implemented frontend preserves this shared identity across sign-in, clinical, patient and administrative screens.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Brand, headings, primary actions | Deep pine | #153F38 |
| Links and emphasis | Muted teal | #276B60 |
| Main background | Warm ivory | #F6F4ED |
| Main text | Charcoal | #202F2C |
| Secondary text | Slate green | #53645F |
| Dividers | Pale sage | #D5DDD6 |
| Surface | White | #FFFFFF |

Use ivory or white text on pine. Use pine, charcoal or teal text on ivory. Dividers are decorative, not text colours. Status labels must always include words and must never rely on colour alone.

## Typography and layout

Arial, with a sans-serif fallback. Bold headings, regular body copy, generous space, consistent left edges. Keep deck copy left aligned to avoid uneven gaps in short lines. Use short sections and plain language. Preserve clinical instructions exactly as supplied by the API.

## Product scope

Clyra is a synthetic-data medication continuity and safe handover project, defined by the supplied Contract v1.3.1 and member briefs. The pitch describes intended behaviour, not verified implementation or clinical outcomes.
